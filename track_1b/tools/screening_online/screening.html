How to screen (read once)
  1. Main photo: OK; or Exclude if a recognisable person, a readable licence plate, a private interior or personal text is visible (tiny, distant people are fine); or Wrong object if it does not show this landmark (another building, a map, a painting).
  2. Other photos: click Use as 2nd view on the best one: the same building, seen clearly, with no recognisable people. If none qualifies, click None usable.
  3. Optional: click Too generic on a photo (no people) that alone could not tell even an expert which object it is: a plain wall, a generic door, a typical interior.

When unsure: Exclude, and don't pick. Tap a photo to see it larger. Working as two? One person starts at card 1, the other at card 133 (use Go to card).
