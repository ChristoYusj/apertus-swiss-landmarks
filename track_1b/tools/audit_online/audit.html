How to grade (read once)

Work alone: choose your ID and grade your items without looking anything up or asking anyone (if several people grade, don't discuss items until everyone has finished). The page never shows the model's grade or the judge's, only your own labels.

For each item compare the model answer with the gold answer and choose:

  • Correct: it names the gold landmark / the right municipality / a year in the window, and nothing contradicts the gold.
  • Incorrect: it commits to something wrong: another landmark, another municipality, a village instead of the municipality, a wrong canton or country, several competing candidates, or (premise question) it accepts the false town.
  • Not attempted: it declines, or stays too vague to check ("a church in the Alps", only the right canton), without contradicting the gold.

Tick used translation if you relied on the English gloss, and unsure if it was a hard call. Every click is saved at once.
