# Consent and data-provenance statement (template)

This dataset contains **no recordings of, or data collected from, human subjects**. Its
content consists of:
- public Wikimedia Commons photographs of buildings and monuments, each under its own free licence;
- facts from Wikidata (CC0);
- questions written by the team;
- responses generated by AI models;
- labels produced by the team members listed below.

The forms below cover the two groups of people whose contributions or likeness could
appear in the data.

## Part B: People who may appear in photographs
Every published photo was screened jointly by the team. Photos showing recognisable people, licence
plates, private interiors or other personal data were **excluded** (not blurred). If you
believe you are recognisable in a photo of this dataset, open a discussion on the
dataset's Hugging Face page with the image ID. The photo is removed in the next version
(within 14 days). It will also be removed from the dataset repository's history where the
platform allows.

## Part C: Rights holders of photographs
All photos are reproduced under their original free licences (CC0, public domain, CC BY,
CC BY-SA or Free Art License). They are downscaled to 500 px and re-encoded without
metadata, as recorded per image together with the author, licence and source link. If you
are the author and want a photo removed, or the attribution corrected, use the same
Hugging Face discussion page. We act within 14 days.

## AI-use statement
The dataset is built for evaluating and improving AI systems. Its content may be used
for training, evaluation and research on AI models, subject to each item's licence
(see `metadata/item_metadata.jsonl` and `metadata/images.jsonl`).
