# Consent and data-provenance statement (template)

This dataset contains **no recordings of, or data collected from, human subjects**. It consists of:
- public Wikimedia Commons photographs of buildings and monuments, each under its own free licence;
- facts from Wikidata (CC0);
- question templates drafted with an AI coding assistant (core templates: French reviewed by team members,
  German/Italian checked by back-translation, Romansh not reviewed; extension templates: no recorded review);
- responses generated by AI models;
- grading labels from an LLM judge and a code grader; human grading labels from one annotator (A1, the team lead)
  on the audit sample; cross-check labels from a second AI (Hermes) and AI-assisted re-reads (Claude);
- photo-screening decisions made by AI (Claude, then a second AI agent, Hermes), with the team lead (the annotator
  below) deciding edge cases; then a third review of every shipped photo by a Gemini agent, whose 14 flags the
  team lead accepted (no person reviewed every shipped photo).

Part A covers the annotator, Part B people who may appear in photos, Part C the photos' rights holders.

## Part B: People who may appear in photographs
Every published photo was screened by AI (two systems), with the team lead deciding edge cases, and then
reviewed again by a third AI system (Gemini), whose flags the team lead accepted. Photos showing
recognisable people, licence plates, private interiors or other personal data were **excluded** (not blurred).
If you think you are recognisable in a photo of this dataset, open a discussion on the dataset's Hugging Face
page with the image ID. The photo is removed in the next version (within 14 days) and, where the platform allows,
from the dataset repository's history.

## Part C: Rights holders of photographs
All photos are reproduced under their original free licences (CC0, public domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA or Free Art
License). They are downscaled to 500 px and re-encoded without metadata; each image record lists these
modifications with the author, licence and source link. If you are the author and want a photo removed or its attribution corrected, use the
same Hugging Face discussion page. We act within 14 days.

## AI-use statement
The dataset is built to evaluate and improve AI systems. Its content may be used for training, evaluation and
research on AI models, subject to each item's licence (see `metadata/item_metadata.jsonl` and
`metadata/images.jsonl`).
